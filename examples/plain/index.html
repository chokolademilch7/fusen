A Small Library in the Hills

Opening hours, borrowing rules, and this month's exhibit.

Opening hours

Open 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. on weekdays, and until 5 p.m. on Saturdays. Closed on Sundays, public holidays, and the last Wednesday of every month.

Borrowing rules

Up to 5 books per person, for 2 weeks. While any of your books are overdue, you cannot borrow new ones.

This month's exhibit

Old local photographs, shown next to the same places as they look today. On the shelves by the window, behind the reading seats.
